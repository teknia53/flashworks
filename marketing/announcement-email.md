# FlashWorks announcement email

**Subject:** FlashWorks is back — free Greek vocabulary flashcards in your browser

**Alternate subjects:**
- Learn your Greek vocabulary anywhere: FlashWorks is now online
- Every word in BBG, one flashcard at a time — free

---

Dear friends,

For years, students have asked me for a simple way to drill their Greek
vocabulary — on the bus, between classes, in the few quiet minutes before the
kids wake up. Today I'm delighted to tell you that FlashWorks, the vocabulary
program that has helped students of *Basics of Biblical Greek* for many years,
now runs right in your web browser.

**FlashWorks** — [billmounce.com/fw](https://billmounce.com/fw)

Here's what you'll find:

- **All 1,140 vocabulary words** from *Basics of Biblical Greek*, organized
  chapter by chapter, so the app always matches where you are in the book.
- **Real flashcards.** See the Greek word, think your answer, then flip the
  card for the meaning — with principal parts for the verbs.
- **Review what you miss.** Words you struggle with come back around until
  they stick.
- **Works everywhere.** Phone, tablet, or computer — if it has a browser, it
  runs FlashWorks. There is nothing to install and no account to create.
- **Completely free.**

Vocabulary is the part of Greek nobody can do for you — but the right tool
makes the daily work lighter. My hope is that FlashWorks turns the spare
corners of your day into steady progress, until the words of the Greek New
Testament start feeling like old friends.

Go to [billmounce.com/fw](https://billmounce.com/fw), pick your chapter, and
start with word one.

For an informed love of God,

Bill Mounce
[billmounce.com](https://www.billmounce.com)
